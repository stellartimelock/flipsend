Phone to PC. No cloud.

Send photos, videos, and files between your Android phone and Windows PC over a direct, encrypted link. Pair once with a QR code. No account.

Coming soon to F-Droid

On an iPhone, this page is the app. Scan, send, done. Nothing is saved after the session.

Relay cap is 100 MB a month, 5 MB a minute, and 48 MB per file in this browser. The 50 GB figure is the shared pool, not yours. It resets on the 1st.

  • Phone to PC, no cloud. Files go straight between your devices. Nothing is stored online, and there is no account.
  • Direct encrypted link. Same Wi-Fi is direct and unlimited. Away from home, the relay passes along ciphertext, about 100 MB a month. Wi-Fi transfers still work when that is used up.
  • Remote desktop. See and use your PC screen from your phone.
  • Works with VPNs. Wi-Fi first, then the encrypted relay. No ads or analytics.
