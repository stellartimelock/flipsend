Terms of use

FlipSend by StellarTimeLock moves files and text between your Android phone, your Windows PC, and this web page.

FlipSend and its companion app Bot Link are free and open source. There is nothing to buy.

Your files go directly between your own devices. We never get a copy. You are responsible for what you send and for keeping your paired devices secure.

Same-Wi-Fi transfers are unlimited. If Wi-Fi cannot reach, FlipSend uses the encrypted relay. That relay is included at no cost.
