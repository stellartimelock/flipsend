FlipSend
Free Privacy Support

Send it to your PC. Keep it yours.

FlipSend moves files and text between your Android phone, your Windows PC, and an iPhone page. Pair with a QR code. Same Wi-Fi is unlimited. No account, ads, or analytics.

Coming soon to F-Droid Download for Windows Connect with iPhone

On an iPhone, Connect with iPhone opens pairing. Scan, choose how to send, done. There is no App Store app and no lasting browser lock.

Unlimited on the same Wi-Fi

Direct transfers on your own network are not metered. If Wi-Fi cannot reach, FlipSend uses the encrypted internet relay, about 100 MB a month. Wi-Fi transfers still work when that allowance is used up.

End-to-end encrypted

The phone and the PC encrypt the session themselves. A relay, if you use one, sees ciphertext only.

No account, ads, or analytics

There is no sign-in and no tracking. FlipSend does not collect a profile of what you send.

Works with VPNs

A VPN address stays in the search list. For a faster direct transfer, turn on the VPN’s allow local network option.

Free

FlipSend is free and open source. Coming soon to F-Droid. Bot Link is a free companion app. Same-Wi-Fi and USB transfers stay unlimited. If those cannot reach, FlipSend uses the encrypted relay, about 100 MB a month. That relay is included at no cost.
